Common questions

What is free and what needs Chand Pro?

Connecting the sensor, live force, your history and exporting your data are free. Measuring, fatigue sessions and workouts need Chand Pro, a monthly subscription with a one-week free trial. You manage or cancel it from your Apple account (Settings → Subscription in the app).

Which sensors work?

Chand works with Bluetooth force sensors that use the Tindeq Progressor protocol. Chand is an independent app and is not affiliated with or endorsed by any sensor manufacturer.

The sensor does not connect.

Turn the sensor on, make sure Bluetooth is on and that Chand has Bluetooth permission (iOS Settings → Chand), close the manufacturer's app if it is connected to the sensor, and tap “Connect sensor” on the Today tab.

Can I try the app without a sensor?

Yes. Turn on Settings → No sensor → Demo mode. “Connect sensor” then connects to a simulated sensor and a “Simulate pull” button appears on the measuring screens.

How do I back up or move my data?

Settings → Data exports the configuration (grips, named measurements, templates and settings) as a JSON file that can be imported again. Results are exported as CSV from the full history.

How do I change the language?

The app follows your iPhone's language (English or Spanish). To choose one just for Chand, go to Settings → Language.

Health notice

Chand is a measuring and training tool, not a medical device. Maximal pulls put a heavy load on fingers, pulleys, tendons and shoulders: warm up, stop if you feel pain and consult a professional if you have an injury or any doubts. You use the app at your own risk.
