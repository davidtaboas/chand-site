Privacy policy

Chand for iPhone · Last updated: 14 November 2026

Chand does not collect any data. The app has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party SDKs, and it never connects to the internet.

What the app stores

Everything you record stays on your iPhone, inside the app: measurements and force curves, sessions, workouts, grips, templates, and the settings and profile you enter (name, body weight, dominant hand). None of it is sent to the developer or to anyone else.

If you have iCloud Backup or an encrypted computer backup enabled, Apple includes the app's data in your device backup, under your own Apple account.

Bluetooth

Chand uses Bluetooth only to connect to your force sensor and read force and battery values from it. It does not use Bluetooth to determine your location or to detect other devices.

Exporting and sharing

You can export your settings (JSON) and results (CSV), or share an image of a result. This only happens when you choose to, through the iOS share sheet, and the file goes wherever you send it.

Deleting your data

You can delete individual items inside the app. Deleting the app from your iPhone removes all of its data.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.

This website

This website uses no cookies, no analytics and no third-party resources, and it stores nothing in your browser. It is hosted on GitHub Pages; as with any website, the hosting provider receives your IP address in order to serve the pages.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with its date.

Contact

Questions about privacy: open an issue on GitHub.
